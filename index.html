Health Tips
Daily wellness advice just for you
💧
Stay Hydrated
Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration helps reduce bloating, ease cramps, and keep your skin glowing.
📅
Track Regularly
Log your cycle every month for at least 3 cycles to get accurate predictions and understand your body's patterns.
🧘‍♀️
Move Your Body
Gentle movement like yoga or walking during your period can significantly reduce cramps and lift your mood.
🥗
Eat Mindfully
Iron-rich foods (spinach, lentils) help during menstruation. Magnesium (dark chocolate, nuts) eases PMS symptoms.
😴
Prioritise Sleep
Aim for 7–9 hours. Your hormones fluctuate across your cycle — honouring your need for more rest is self-care.
🩺
See a Doctor
If you experience extremely painful periods, very irregular cycles, or unusual symptoms — always consult a healthcare professional.
